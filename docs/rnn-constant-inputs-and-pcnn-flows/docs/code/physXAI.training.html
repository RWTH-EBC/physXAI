physXAI.training.recursive module

Training of single step models on the multi step (recursive) error.

A single step model trained on the single step error can still drift when it is applied recursively in an MPC. Training the recursive rollout (physXAI.evaluation.rollout) directly on the error over a horizon counters that. The first step is weighted more, since it is the most relevant one for the MPC (it is applied to the plant); the following steps keep the model stable over the horizon.

>>> m = ClassicalANNModel(recursive_training=RecursiveTraining(horizon='24h', first_step_weight=0.25))

Only the weights of the step model are trained, so the saved model stays a single step model, which AgentLib-MPC uses as before.

class physXAI.training.recursive.RecursiveTraining(horizon=None, first_step_weight=0.25, step_weights=None, mode='single_then_multi', epochs=None, learning_rate=None, batch_size=None, stride=1, early_stopping_epochs=None)[source]

Bases: object

Settings of the training on the multi step error.

Parameters:

  • horizon (int | str | None) – Training horizon in steps or as time delta string (‘24h’). Defaults to the evaluation horizon of the training data.

  • first_step_weight (float) – Share of the first step in the loss (the other steps share the rest equally). Ignored if step_weights is given.

  • step_weights (list[float] | None) – Optional weight per step (normalized to a sum of one).

  • mode (Mode) – ‘single_then_multi’ (default: train on the single step error first, then fine-tune on the multi step error) or ‘multi_step’ (only the multi step error).

  • epochs (int | None) – Epochs of the multi step training. Defaults to the epochs of the model.

  • learning_rate (float | None) – Learning rate of the multi step training. Defaults to the one of the model, for the fine-tuning of ‘single_then_multi’ to a tenth of it. After a least squares solution (ModularANN(solver='ols')) of an unnormalized Linear block, the weights are small and need a much smaller learning rate (e.g. 1e-6).

  • batch_size (int | None) – Windows per batch. Defaults to the batch size of the model.

  • stride (int) – Distance between two training windows in steps.

  • early_stopping_epochs (int | None) – Patience of the early stopping on the validation loss. Defaults to the one of the model.
