physXAI.models.ann.ann_design module

Keras models of physXAI: single step networks (ClassicalANNModel, CMNNModel, RBFModel, LinANNModel, PINNModel, base class ANNModel) and recurrent multi step models (RNNModel, PCNNModel).

All of them share the training settings (epochs, learning rate, batch size, early stopping, seed); single step networks can additionally be trained on the multi step error (recursive_training) and with a physics residual (physics).

Bases: ANNModel

Physics-informed neural network: a CMNN trained on the data and on physics targets.

A physics target is a data column computed from a physical model of the output, e.g. a simplified heat pump model columns={'pinn': expression} in the preprocessing. The model has a single output (the data output) and is used and exported like any other model; the physics targets only enter the loss (see physXAI.models.ann.pinn.physics):

L = mse(y_hat, y) / s + sum_i w_i * mse(y_hat, p_i) / s, s = var(y_train)

Physics targets are taken at the predicted time, like the output. For a model predicting Change(T) with a physics model of the step T(t) -> T(t+1), the column of the physics prediction computed from the data at time t has to be shifted back by one step: columns={'dT_rc': expression.lag(1)}.

Example

>>> prep = PreprocessingSingleStep(inputs=[...], output='P_el', columns={'pinn': physics})
>>> m = PINNModel(physics_targets={'pinn': 0.5})
>>> td = prep.pipeline(path, model=m)   # adds the physics targets as loss columns
>>> m.pipeline(td)

class physXAI.models.ann.ann_design.RNNModel(rnn_units=32, rnn_layer='RNN', epochs=1000, learning_rate=0.001, batch_size=32, early_stopping_epochs=100, random_seed=None, feature_architecture=None, loss_on='trajectory', first_step_weight=None, step_weights=None, **kwargs)[source]

Bases: MultiStepModel

Recurrent neural network (SimpleRNN, GRU or LSTM) for multi step predictions.

The model warms itself up: starting from zero states, it is applied to the warmup_width measured steps in front of the prediction, which are not part of the loss. AgentLib-MPC warms the model up the same way, so there is no separate initialization model.

Predicting the change of a feature (output px.Change('T')) is recommended. The trajectory of T is then the measured start value plus the accumulated changes, and the loss is computed on that trajectory (loss_on='trajectory'). If T is also an input of the model, the predicted T is fed back during the prediction (closed loop), as in an MPC.

The saved model (save_model, save_for_agentlib) is the step model [sequence, *states] -> [prediction, *states], which AgentLib-MPC evaluates one step at a time.

Parameters:

  • rnn_units (int) – Number of units of the recurrent layer.

  • rnn_layer (str) – Type of the recurrent layer (‘RNN’, ‘GRU’ or ‘LSTM’).

  • epochs (int) – Number of training epochs.

  • learning_rate (float) – Learning rate of the Adam optimizer.

  • batch_size (int) – Number of sequences per batch.

  • early_stopping_epochs (int | None) – Patience of the early stopping on the validation loss (None disables it).

  • random_seed (int | None) – Seed of the weight initialization and the batch order, set when the model is generated. Defaults to the global seed (physXAI.set_seed), else a new seed is drawn (stored in the config).

  • feature_architecture (Expr | list[physXAI.expr.core.Expr] | dict | None) – An expression (physXAI.expr), or a list of them, building the inputs of the recurrent layer out of the input features. Variables are not normalized, wrap them in px.Normalize where needed.

  • loss_on (str) – ‘trajectory’ (default): loss on the trajectory of the predicted features, i.e. on the accumulated changes. ‘outputs’: loss on the outputs (e.g. the changes) themselves.

  • first_step_weight (float | None) – Optional share of the first predicted step in the loss, the other steps share the rest. None weights all steps equally.

  • step_weights (list[float] | None) – Optional weight per predicted step (overrides first_step_weight).

class physXAI.models.ann.ann_design.PCNNModel(heating=None, cooling=None, losses=None, rnn_units=32, rnn_layer='LSTM', **kwargs)[source]

Bases: RNNModel

Physically consistent neural network (PCNN, Di Natale et al. 2022, Applied Energy 325, 119806) for the multi step prediction of a temperature.

The temperature T is the sum of two states: an energy accumulator E, a linear physics-inspired module driven by the heating and cooling inputs and the heat losses to the outside (or neighbouring zones), and the unforced dynamics D, a recurrent network f on D and the remaining inputs (e.g. solar radiation):

E(k+1) = E(k) + sum a * u_heating(k) - sum d * u_cooling(k) + sum b * (T_x(k) - T(k)) D(k+1) = D(k) + f(D(k), x(k)), T = D + E

Since D does not depend on the controls and T_x, the prediction is physically consistent over the whole horizon for any f: T increases with heating, decreases with cooling and increases with T_x, if the parameters a, b, d are positive (enforced) and 1 - sum b > 0 per time step (checked after the training).

The data are PreprocessingMultiStep sequences with the output px.Change('T') and T as input; T is fed back during the prediction (closed loop), the model warms up on the measured past, and the parameters are trained with the network on the error of the trajectory over the horizon. The saved step model is exported to AgentLib-MPC like an RNN.

The parameters start at initial rates per hour, scaled to the time step; the network learns a factor on them. A rate is either a physical rule of thumb (a heating rate in K/h at u = 1, e.g. “+1 K in 2 h at full power”: 0.5, a loss rate in 1/h, e.g. “-1.5 K in 6 h when it is 25 K colder outside”: 1.5 / 25 / 6 = 0.01) or None: estimated from the training data by nonnegative least squares of the single step energy balance T(k+1) - T(k) = sum a * u_heating - sum d * u_cooling + sum b * (T_x - T) + c on the steps of the training split (the offset c belongs to the unforced dynamics D). The initial rates are reported in td.physics['initial_rates'].

Parameters:

  • heating (dict[str, float] | None) – {input: initial heating rate in K/h per unit of the input, or None (from the data)}.

  • cooling (dict[str, float] | None) – {input: initial cooling rate in K/h per unit of the input, or None} (the input is a positive cooling power).

  • losses (dict[str, float] | None) – {temperature input: initial loss rate in 1/h, or None}.

  • rnn_units (int) – Units of the recurrent layer of f.

  • rnn_layer (str) – ‘LSTM’ (default), ‘GRU’ or ‘RNN’.

  • **kwargs – Training settings of RNNModel (epochs, learning_rate, batch_size, early_stopping_epochs, random_seed, loss_on, first_step_weight, step_weights).

Example

>>> prep = PreprocessingMultiStep(inputs=['u_hp', 'T_amb', 'solar', 'T'], output=px.Change('T'),
...                               warmup_width='3h', label_width='12h')
>>> m = PCNNModel(heating={'u_hp': 0.5}, losses={'T_amb': 0.01})   # rules of thumb
>>> m = PCNNModel(heating={'u_hp': None}, losses={'T_amb': None})  # estimated from the training data
