1""" 

2Modular artificial neural networks: a trainable model built from a `physXAI.expr` graph. 

3 

4 >>> from physXAI import expr as px 

5 >>> v = px.Vars() 

6 >>> model = ModularANN(px.Linear([v.mDot, v.load, v.mDot * (v.T_in - v.T), v.T]), solver="ols") 

7 

8Physical arithmetic (like the heat flow above) is part of the keras model. It is therefore 

9recomputed in every step of a recursive prediction and exported to AgentLib-MPC together with 

10the model, so the MPC only needs the raw inputs. 

11"""

46 """ 

47 Trainable keras model defined by an expression graph. 

48 

49 Args: 

50 architecture: The expression computing the output, e.g. `px.Linear([...])`. 

51 rescale_output: Rescale the output with mean and standard deviation of the training targets. 

52 solver: 'adam' (gradient descent), 'ols' (closed form least squares) or 'qp' (constrained least squares: 

53 sign bounds of `Linear(monotonicities=...)` and `shape`). 'ols' and 'qp' need a `Linear` block on data 

54 only inputs without normalization as architecture. 

55 shape: Optional `ShapeConstraints`: monotonicity and convexity of the prediction with respect to raw data 

56 columns, guaranteed on a domain (requires solver='qp', see `physXAI.models.modular.shape_constraints`). 

57 The result is stored in `td.physics['shape_constraints']`. 

58 batch_size: Batch size of the training. 

59 epochs: Maximum number of epochs. 

60 learning_rate: Learning rate of the Adam optimizer. 

61 early_stopping_epochs: Patience of the early stopping on the validation loss (None: no early stopping). 

62 random_seed: Seed of the weights and the batch order, set when the model is generated. Defaults to the global 

63 seed (`physXAI.set_seed`), else a new seed is drawn (stored in the config). 

64 warm_start: With solver='adam' and a normalized `Linear` block as architecture: the training starts at the 

65 least squares solution of its data only terms (sign bounds of its monotonicities included), the 

66 trainable terms (e.g. a `px.MLP` for the remaining dynamics) start with weight 0. The model starts 

67 as the linear model and the network learns its residual, which makes the training independent of 

68 a bad initialization. No effect for other architectures. 

69 """
